About This Application

What is Crime Rate Prediction?

Crime rate prediction has become an important tool for law enforcement agencies to better understand patterns of crime and anticipate where crime is likely to occur. By predicting future crime trends, agencies can allocate resources more effectively to areas with anticipated increases in criminal activity.

Our Dataset

We use comprehensive data from the Indian National Crime Rate Bureau (NCRB) covering 19 metropolitan cities from 2014 to 2021. This includes statistics on 10 different crime categories including murder, kidnapping, cybercrime, economic offences, and crimes against specific populations.

The Technology

Our system uses scikit-learn's Random Forest Regression model—an ensemble learning technique that creates multiple decision trees and averages their predictions for superior accuracy. This approach achieves a remarkable 93.20% accuracy on test datasets.
